# James Burchill

I build and guide technology that helps organizations understand what is changing, make better decisions, and act with greater confidence.

That work includes [Driftinel](https://driftinel.com)—an operational-awareness product designed to help organizations recognize meaningful drift, preserve context, and act before emerging problems become expensive surprises.

The objective is not more technology, more dashboards, or AI for its own sake. It is clearer awareness, better organizational memory, and earlier opportunities to act.

## Public work

- [Driftinel](https://driftinel.com) — operational awareness for fewer surprises.
- [Because Drift Happens](https://becausedrifthappens.com) — the doctrine behind the work.
- [The Vault](https://vault.jamesburchill.com) — field notes, working ideas, and evidence from the journey.

Most of the underlying technology and experimental work is intentionally private. I publish the ideas and outcomes that matter—not every mechanism used to produce them.
